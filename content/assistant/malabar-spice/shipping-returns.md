We deliver across India with tracked courier services. Every order gets a tracking link by email and SMS once it is shipped.

## Delivery times

- Kerala: 2–3 working days after dispatch.
- Rest of South India: 3–5 working days.
- Rest of India: 5–8 working days.
- Orders placed before 2 pm IST on a working day are dispatched the same day; later orders are dispatched the next working day.

## Shipping charges

- Free shipping on orders of ₹799 or more.
- Orders below ₹799: flat ₹60 shipping charge.
- Cash on delivery orders have an extra ₹40 handling fee.

## Returns and refunds

You can return unopened products within 7 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Opened spice packs cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, unless they are damaged or wrong (see below). To start a return, email help@malabarspice.example with your order number; we arrange the pickup. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after we receive the item. Shipping charges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

## Damaged or wrong items

If a product arrives damaged, leaking, or is not what you ordered, email a photo to help@malabarspice.example within 48 hours of delivery. We send a free replacement or a full refund including shipping charges — your choice. You do not need to send the damaged item back.

## Delayed or lost parcels

If your tracking has not updated for 5 working days, contact us and we will investigate with the courier. Parcels confirmed lost are replaced or refunded in full.

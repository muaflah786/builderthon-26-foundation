Malabar Spice Market is a small family business in Kozhikode, Kerala, started in 2019. We sell single-origin spices bought directly from about 40 small farming families in Wayanad, Idukki, Alappuzha and Kollam.

## How we source

We visit farms before each harvest and pay farmers a fixed price agreed before the season, above the local market rate. We buy only whole, sun-dried spices and do all grinding and blending ourselves in Kozhikode.

## How we pack

Spices are packed in small weekly batches in food-grade, resealable pouches with a packing date. Gift boxes use reusable tins. We do not add colours, preservatives or anti-caking agents.

## Certifications

We are registered with FSSAI (food safety licence). Our spices are not certified organic.

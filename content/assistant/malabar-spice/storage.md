## Storage tips

Keep spices in the resealable pouch or an airtight jar, away from sunlight, heat and steam (not right above the stove). Do not use a wet spoon. Whole spices keep their aroma about twice as long as ground spices; grind small amounts as needed. Refrigeration is not needed and can add moisture.

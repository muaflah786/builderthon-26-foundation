## Placing an order

1. Open the Shop page and choose a product.
2. On the product page, choose the quantity and select "Add to cart".
3. Open the cart (top right) and select "Checkout".
4. Enter your delivery address and phone number, or sign in to use a saved address.
5. Choose a payment method: UPI, card, net banking or cash on delivery (orders up to ₹2,000).
6. Confirm the order. You receive an order number and confirmation email immediately.

Note: in this demo website the cart and checkout are not implemented; the steps describe how the real shop works.

## Tracking an order

Use the tracking link in your shipping email or SMS. If you lost it, email help@malabarspice.example with your order number.
